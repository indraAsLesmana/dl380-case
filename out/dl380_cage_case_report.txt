 NOTES
   * The 92 mm fan frame is taller than the 87.8 mm bay bore, so the
     rear section steps up 8.2 mm to hold it inside.
     The largest fan that would keep the top flat is 83.8 mm.
   * Air path: open front -> caddies -> plenum -> fan -> honeycomb grille.
     No tapered duct any more: with the fan inside the plenum there is
     nothing to funnel into, and the volume is needed for cabling.
   * The grille is punched through a 3.0 mm membrane, not the full 8.4 mm
     wall, so it costs little airflow and still looks clean.
   * Cage side-screw Z positions are a starting suggestion: drill/print
     only the pair that lines up with your cage's own holes.
   * Rear stop frame inner aperture: 137.8 x 79.8 mm
