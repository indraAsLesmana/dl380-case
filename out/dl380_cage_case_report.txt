 FAN HOUSING  - it slides in from the top and sets tight with no screws

                             the screw holes line up if you ever use them

   behind (Z+)             : the rear wall / grille face
   forward (Z-)            : two front corner tabs, 16.0 mm tall - the fan
                             cannot tip forward past them
   up (Y+)                 : two fins on the lid's underside reach down to
                             y=97.0, 1.0 mm above the frame's top edge
   => restrained on four sides by the case and on the fifth by the lid

   On the rails: they are a SNUG fit, not a press fit, and that is
   deliberate.  A rigid slot narrower than the fan frame cannot be
   inserted into at all - the fan jams at the top - so an interference
   fit is geometrically impossible here.  0.2 mm per side is what stops
   it rattling.  Tune it with FAN_GUIDE_CLEAR, and set FAN_LID_GAP to
   change how much the fan can lift.  The 4 x O4.2 screw holes are still
   there if you would rather bolt it.

 NOTES
   * The 92 mm fan frame is taller than the 87.8 mm bay bore, so the
     rear section steps up 8.2 mm to hold it inside.
     The largest fan that would keep the top flat is 83.8 mm.
   * Air path: open front -> caddies -> plenum -> fan -> honeycomb grille.
     No tapered duct any more: with the fan inside the plenum there is
     nothing to funnel into, and the volume is needed for cabling.
   * The grille is punched through a 3.0 mm membrane, not the full 8.4 mm
     wall, so it costs little airflow and still looks clean.
   * Cage side-screw Z positions are a starting suggestion: drill/print
     only the pair that lines up with your cage's own holes.
   * Rear stop frame inner aperture: 137.8 x 79.8 mm
